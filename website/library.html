Training Manual

Goal-based workout guidance for paid members.

CFJ includes a training guide inspired by strength-training anatomy principles. The manual is organised by what each member wants to achieve.

What is the training manual?

The CFJ training manual is a member-only guide that turns each member's goal into practical workout instructions. It explains what to train, why the muscle group matters, how to perform the movement safely, and how to progress without rushing.

What inspired it?

The guide was inspired by the exercise and anatomy resources included in the project pack. Instead of copying book content, CFJ uses the same idea: members learn which body parts are involved and receive clear, safe instructions matched to goals such as strength, weight loss, muscle tone, or endurance.

Select Your Training Goals

New members can choose the goals they want to focus on. Your selected goals appear first, and other guides appear underneath.

Goal-based training manuals are unlocked after current-month payment.
