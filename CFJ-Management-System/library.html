What is the training manual?

The CFJ training manual is a member-only guide that turns each member's goal into practical workout instructions. It explains what to train, why the muscle group matters, how to perform the movement safely, and how to progress without rushing.

What inspired it?

These original plans use the supplied training images as visual references. Each guide lists equipment, a session length, exercise steps and pacing suggestions. A trainer can adapt the plan to your needs.
