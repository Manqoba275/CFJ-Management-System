About The System

Your fitness journey starts here.

Member App

Members can sign up, log in, pay monthly fees, book classes, view payment confirmations, and access training guides after payment.

Admin Portal

Admins can log in to view registered members, joined dates, payment records, paid months, and confirmation messages.

Training Manual

The guide is divided by member goals such as strength, weight loss, muscle tone, and endurance. It is unlocked for paid members.

Communication

After a payment is recorded, the system generates a simulated SMS and email confirmation for the member's payment section.

Why CFJ Needed This

Less paperwork. Better service. Clear reports.

CFJ brings the gym experience into one place: members get faster service, staff spend less time on paperwork, and management can make decisions from clear, current information.

For Members

Members can register, manage preferences, pay fees, book classes, connect with trainers, and unlock their training manual after payment.

For Staff

Staff can record attendance manually, process payments, monitor paid months, and support members with fast searchable information.

For Management

Admins get live member and payment reports showing joined dates, invoices, monthly payment status, and confirmation messages.
