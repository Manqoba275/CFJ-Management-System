Why We Host It

Running events give members a clear goal outside normal gym sessions. They build community, improve consistency, and help people experience progress in a measurable way.

CFJ also supports members with blood pressure related problems by encouraging responsible cardio training, warm-ups, hydration, pacing, and regular check-ins before high-intensity effort.

Preparation

Members receive pacing advice, endurance sessions, mobility work, and recovery reminders before the event.

Health Awareness

Participants are encouraged to monitor blood pressure, hydrate properly, and train at a safe intensity.

Community

The marathon brings CFJ members together and gives beginners a supportive reason to keep going.
